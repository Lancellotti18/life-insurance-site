What Happens Next

What to Expect on the Call

We keep it simple, transparent, and respectful of your time. Here's exactly how the conversation goes.

👋

Quick Introductions (2 min)

We'll start by learning a little about you — your background, where you're at professionally, and what's motivated you to explore this opportunity.

🔍

The Opportunity Overview (8 min)

A team leader walks you through how the system works — how agents get leads, how policies are written, how commissions are paid, and what income timelines realistically look like.

❓

Your Questions Answered (8 min)

This is your time. Ask anything — about the income potential, the licensing process, the time commitment, or anything else on your mind. Nothing is off-limits.

🚀

Next Steps (2 min)

If it feels like a fit, we'll talk about how to move forward. If it's not the right time or the right match, we'll be upfront about that too. No hard sell, ever.

Ready to Apply Without the Call?

If you've already done your research and you're ready to take the next step, go ahead and apply directly. It takes less than 5 minutes and we'll reach out to schedule an onboarding call either way.
