The Case for Joining

Why Join My Team?

Most people have never considered life insurance sales as a career. Here's why that's a mistake — and why our team specifically changes the game.

💸

Industry-Leading Commission Structure

We offer some of the highest commission rates in the industry — starting agents earn significantly more than the national average. As you grow and develop your own team, your override income creates a second income stream that can eventually surpass your direct sales income entirely.

🎯

Warm, Inbound Leads — Never Cold Call

Forget cold calling strangers. Every client we work with has already requested information about life insurance coverage. Our proprietary lead system connects you with people who genuinely want to hear from you. This alone is worth everything — it's why our agents close at a far higher rate than the industry average.

⚡

48-Hour Commission Payouts

Most traditional insurance agencies pay out commissions monthly or even quarterly. We pay within 48 hours of a policy being issued — and often same-day. You work hard, you get paid fast. It's that simple. No waiting, no wondering when the check arrives.

🏠

100% Remote — Work From Anywhere

Our entire operation is built to run remotely. All meetings, training, client consultations, and paperwork happen virtually. Whether you're at home, traveling, or working from a coffee shop, your business comes with you. All you need is a phone, a laptop, and Wi-Fi.

📚

Full Training & Licensing Support — No Experience Needed

You don't need a background in insurance, finance, or sales to succeed here. We provide step-by-step guidance through the licensing process (which most people complete in 1–2 weeks), followed by structured sales training, product knowledge, and ongoing coaching. We've successfully trained people from virtually every professional background.

📈

Unlimited Earning Potential & Growth

There's no cap, quota ceiling, or glass ceiling here. Your income is directly tied to your output. Top agents on our team earn $10,000–$30,000+ per month. As you grow, you have the option to recruit and build your own team — unlocking override income that pays you whether you personally write a policy that day or not.

🤝

Supportive Team Culture That Actually Shows Up

We do weekly team calls, one-on-one mentorship, shared resources, group chats, and team leaderboards that celebrate every win. When you join, you're not handed a PDF and told good luck. You're welcomed into a community of agents who want to see you win — because when you win, we all win.
